7. Campaign Creation and Planning Workflow

Step 1 — UCI Registration/Login

1.  UCI registers himself/herself.
2.  Registration attributes will be provided by the project owner.
3.  UCI logs in.
4.  UCI lands on the UCI dashboard.
5.  UCI can:
    -   Create/run a new campaign.
    -   Select a past campaign to view historical data.

Step 2 — Create New Campaign

The UCI creates a fresh campaign.

Campaign attributes will be supplied separately.

For every new campaign:

-   New micro plans are created.
-   New campaign-specific assignments are created.
-   New campaign-specific sheets are created.
-   Previous campaign data remains historical and is not reused as
    editable current-campaign data.

Step 3 — Add/Select AICs

The UCI can:

-   Register new AICs.
-   Select already-registered AICs from previous campaigns.

The selected AICs become associated with the new campaign.

Step 4 — Divide UC into Areas

The UCI divides the Union Council into areas.

Example:

-   Area 1 → AIC 1
-   Area 2 → AIC 2
-   Area N → AIC N

Each area’s exact attributes will be supplied later.

The system must prevent unauthorized access across assigned areas.

Step 5 — Create UCMP

The UCI creates the campaign’s UCMP.

The UCI can:

-   Save Draft.
-   Continue editing while editable.
-   Finalize.

After finalization:

-   UCMP becomes locked.
-   It becomes read-only.
-   Its finalized version must be preserved.

Step 6 — Notify AICs

After the required UCI planning work is completed, the UCI can notify
AICs to begin their planning work.
